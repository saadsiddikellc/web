About Fred

A Specialist Mindset.
Not a Parts-Swapping Approach.

I’m Fred, automotive electrical specialist at Fred’s Auto. I diagnose and repair complex and advanced electrical faults that leave other shops guessing.

Most electrical problems are not hard to fix. They are hard to find. So I work systematically — establish the symptom, test the circuit, follow the evidence — rather than replacing parts and hoping the fault disappears.

That approach has kept vehicles reliably on the road that others had written off. I also perform thorough pre-purchase electrical inspections, so you understand a vehicle’s electrical condition before you commit to it.

  • Specialist automotive electrical knowledge
  • Complex and advanced fault diagnosis
  • Systematic fault finding, not trial and error
